## 📖 About The Project

**"What is the universe made of?"** is the primary question humanity has pursued for over 2,600 years.

This repository provides an interactive and academic journey tracing how the conception of reality evolved from **"Matter"** (Atoms/Substance) to **"Energy and Fields"** (Quantum Field Theory, $E=mc^2$) and ultimately to **"Information and Computation"** (Digital Physics, *It from Bit*, Holographic Principle, Simulation Hypothesis).

---

## 🔬 Interactive Simulation Laboratories

1. **Atomic Models & Quantum Orbitals Simulator:** Real-time visual comparison between Democritus's indivisible sphere, Bohr orbits, and Schrödinger probability clouds.
2. **Double Slit & Wavefunction Collapse:** Photon interference patterns and observer detection collapse mechanics.
3. **Wolfram Cellular Automata:** Rule 30 (deterministic chaos), Rule 110 (Turing complete), Rule 90 (fractal), and Rule 184 (traffic flow).
4. **Qubit & Bloch Sphere:** Quantum state vector $|\Psi\rangle = \alpha|0\rangle + \beta|1\rangle$, Hadamard $H$, Pauli $X, Z$ gates and probability distributions.
5. **Black Hole & Holographic Calculator:** Compute Schwarzschild radius, event horizon area, Bekenstein-Hawking entropy, Planck pixel count, and bit storage.
6. **Bostrom's Simulation Argument Trilemma:** Dynamic Bayesian simulation probability analyzer: $P(\text{Sim}) = \frac{f_p N_I}{f_p N_I + 1}$.
